# Abstract

CARVid helps editors adapt landscape videos with on-screen graphics to new aspect ratios while preserving readability and design intent. It combines graphics decomposition, text-aware scaling, layout-aware segmentation, and content-aware recomposition to support automatic and user-directed retargeting.
