# Abstract

Map2Video grounds AI video generation in real-world locations. It lets creators annotate character trajectories on maps and directly manipulate cameras and character masks in geodetic coordinates. In a study with twelve filmmakers, it improved controllability, reduced cognitive effort, and strengthened spatial consistency across shots compared with an image-to-video baseline.
